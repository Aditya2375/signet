    Engine: MediaPipe landmarkers + a transformer trained on WLASL-100, running in WebAssembly on this device. The video stream is processed locally and is never recorded or sent anywhere.

    The hundred words

    The vocabulary is the WLASL-100 subset - the one hundred most-covered signs in the World-Level ASL dataset. Each word links to a dictionary entry if you want to learn the sign before testing the model on it.

      Under the hood

      It runs in three parts: landmark detectors that turn video into numbers, a transformer that turns the numbers into a word, and a small state machine that decides when a sign happened.

      1 · See

      MediaPipe hand and pose landmarkers look at each webcam frame and return 75 points for both hands and the upper body, as plain coordinates.

      →
      2 · Remember

      The last couple of seconds of points become a 48-step sequence, resampled so fast and slow signs line up.

      →
      3 · Read

      A 3-layer transformer encoder (~950k parameters, ONNX, WebAssembly) scores the sequence against all 100 signs. Smoothing and a threshold decide what goes on the tape.

      What it is not

      • Not sentence-level. It reads one isolated sign at a time. Real ASL grammar - facial markers, spatial referencing, fingerspelling - is out of scope.
      • American Sign Language only. Other sign languages (ISL, BSL, LSF…) use different vocabularies and grammars; this model knows none of them.
      • Not a medical or accessibility-critical tool. It is a learning project. The numbers above are dataset numbers and it is far from perfect; top-5 is there so you can see what it almost said.
      • Camera-dependent. Bad light, cluttered backgrounds and cropped hands cost accuracy, exactly as they cost you accuracy when reading signs on a bad video call.

      Privacy, in one paragraph

      There is no backend. The page is static files, and whoever hosts it sees ordinary page requests like any web server. The models download once and run in WebAssembly on your device. The webcam stream is handed by the browser directly to the page and discarded frame by frame. No video, landmarks, predictions or usage data are recorded, stored or transmitted. Once the page and the models have loaded, you can pull the network cable and it keeps working.
